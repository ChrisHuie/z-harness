When authoring a new seam that REUSES a hardened contract's pattern, inheriting the VOCABULARY is not inheriting the MECHANISMS. Concrete case (2026-07-12): `sink-verify.md` reused `payment-adapter.md`'s framing ("double-book ≡ double-pay", verify-as-fact, three-verdict classifier) but dropped **PC5's three actual mechanisms** — content-derived key · committed-before-apply · one-per-slot FREEZE — kept `query_and_retry`'s re-apply-if-not on sinks where absence is unprovable, and let the weakest sink (AdCP) assert no economics while reading the wrong endpoint. A 2-lens red-team caught a real **double-book** (crash/race) and a **wrong `BookingFact` reaching the money path**; fixed via SV1–SV8. This was the **second consecutive seam** (after payment-adapter itself) with the same class of gap.

**Why:** a design that says "own the mechanism" reads correct *precisely because* it uses the right words; the adversarial pass is what forces each borrowed CLAIM to become an enforceable predicate/invariant/guard. The red-team has caught a real hole in **every** money/money-adjacent contract drafted so far (delivery-oracle F1–5, payment-adapter PC1–7, sink-verify SV1–8, model-layer ML1–8, I15/RateAuthority self-issuance, **reference-agents RA1–6/RA-BENE**, **authority-layer RT1–9** — the **8th**, again my own hour-old synthesis: the delegation model borrowed UCAN "attenuation" / macaroon "caveats" vocabulary while dropping the mechanisms (a direction-blind subset check that inverts for floor/exclusion-sets; "signature-links" naming neither the prf-chain nor HMAC-caveat-chaining), and "one root per fact-type" resolved KIND not specific-controller → two CRITICALs, [[project-authority-layer]]; **9th** = **technical-grading GT1–7** — even a FOCUSED 1-lens pass on a MOSTLY-CONSOLIDATION doc found real holes because the body overclaimed relative to its own residuals (the grader can't tell a real third-party agent from a schema-valid sandbox-conformant shell), [[project-technical-grading]]; **10th = I16 mandate-principal binding** — the live identity leg is degenerate (the operator is the *agent*, the mandate names the *principal* — `agent≠principal` for any delegated buy), so binding *enforcement* is coupled to the delegation chain (RT7), NOT standalone, [[project-two-seat-model]]; **11th = T15 constructive creative-readiness** — a TOCTOU between reaction-enqueue and drain (freeze-wins is money-path-ONLY, so SCHEDULED lands on expired inventory), `verified`≠two-witnesses (re-opens RA3), approval bound to `creative_id` not a content hash, [[project-transition-as-trigger]]) — it is load-bearing, not ceremony.

**NEW meta-lesson (2026-07-13, the 10th/11th):** a fresh adversary corrects the **SCOPING**, not just a mechanism. Both I16 and T15 were pitched to the user as *small, standalone, ours-to-author-now* kernel invariants; the fresh red-team showed **both couple to deferred/OPEN work** (I16→RT7 delegation chain + durable principal-identity plumbing + RT4; T15→RA3 + per-package `ReservationHold` + companion/render modeling). So "we identified the right invariant" and "we can bank it standalone" are DIFFERENT claims — the adversary is what tells them apart. Author the *specification* (valuable — it names the requirement), but do NOT promise standalone bankability until the adversary has probed the coupling. **The 7th time (2026-07-13, reference-agents) it went RECURSIVE:** a fresh 3-lens adversary showed my OWN first-pass red-team (RA1–RA4) had itself mirrored the hardened independence prose and overclaimed (an `I16` "provably distinct beneficial owners" with no *prover*; a lineage tag the attacker controls). A synthesizer cannot red-team their own synthesis. The content lesson from that pass — independence proven CRYPTOGRAPHICALLY (key/ownership-disjoint) is NOT the INTEREST-independence corroboration needs (seats are safe as adversaries; participants are capturable by whoever pays them) — is in [[project-reference-agents]].

**How to apply:** for any new money or money-adjacent contract — and ESPECIALLY one that claims to mirror a hardened one — run an adversarial red-team (1–3 lenses by stakes) BEFORE banking, **and the adversary must be a FRESH agent, not the synthesizer** (the recursive lesson: your own first-pass hardening will itself mirror the prose it borrowed — spawn independent lenses that attack your fixes for bypasses, don't accept them). When a doc reuses a hardened pattern, explicitly verify that each borrowed claim is backed by the SAME mechanism (key derivation, commit-ordering, invariant guard, trust-root binding), not merely the same sentence. Ask per claim: "where is the predicate/invariant/test that makes this true, and is it the same one the parent contract shipped?"
