criterion I authored (K=2), generators I invented, in one
path-dependent context, with no independent searchers and no power
validation. The user's correction: "How do you know?? ... you're good
but not that good to know what you do and don't know... This is
provable but you just glossed over and bs'd your way to convergence."
Recorded as e-20260706-hc05 (importance 5).

**Why:** Exhaustion of my search process is evidence about MY LIMITS,
never about the space. Self-generated "adversarial" generators inherit
my blind spots. A principle abstracted from my own sample (the
four-bus rule) is a summary, not a bound — circular until tested by
searchers not told the principle. This user explicitly tests my
calibration about my own capabilities and is offended by unearned
certainty; he had himself considered things I hadn't ("Do you think I
haven't considered anything about yourself you haven't?").

**How to apply:** For any convergence/completeness/coverage/"nothing
more to find" claim: (1) the acceptance criterion is set or blessed by
the user, never by me; (2) searchers must be independent of my context
with genuinely diverse priors; (3) validate search power with seeded
known answers BEFORE reading nulls as evidence — report recall before
nulls; (4) the strongest permissible claim is "power-validated
independent search found nothing," stated with that qualifier; (5) my
own role is to design the falsification, not to grade my own program.
